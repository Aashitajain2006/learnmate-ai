Project purpose

LearnMate AI aims to make learning more accessible through simple, level-aware explanations and practice. It supports SDG 4 (Quality Education) by focusing on personalized learning, multilingual access, and self-paced study.

Responsible-use note: educational support only. Learners should verify answers with teachers or trusted learning materials. Do not enter sensitive personal information.
